    First author · IRMMW-THz 2026 (paper and poster)

    Ansys HFSS · DWL 66+ lithography · ITO sputtering · CW THz photomixing

    Optically tunable THz metasurface on Fe-doped Ga₂O₃

    An 808 nm pump turns a resonator array on Fe:β-Ga2O3 from absorber to mirror, with no contacts or bias: 95% reflected-power modulation at 300 GHz in simulation and 86% switching measured on the bare crystal. I fabricated the first ITO array on the crystal; every cell survived lift-off and its transmission matches HFSS to 0.04 rms.

    808 nm CW laser · 10 kV bench · photocurrent analysis

    10 kV β-Ga₂O₃ photoconductive switches

    Fe impurity states let an 808 nm laser trigger a 10 kV switch below the bandgap: on/off ratio above 15, photocurrent linear in laser power at every bias, and the electrothermal limits mapped. I worked on the 808 nm photoconduction measurements.

    EIT-like terahertz metasurface, a full-wave design study

    A published EIT metasurface rebuilt in HFSS to within 0.7% on the fundamental dip, 5.3 ps of group delay in a 5 µm film, then extended: 100 nm of aluminum is enough, BCB beats polyimide by 14%, and a gated graphene layer switches the window with 45.8% modulation depth.

    First author · manuscript in preparation · MRS Fall 2026 poster

    Deep-UV photoluminescence · LN₂ cryostat · LA-ICP-MS · XRD · ellipsometry

    Excitation-selective trace rare-earth luminescence in GdScO₃

    Nominally undoped GdScO₃ substrates emit sharp lines from Eu³⁺ and Tb³⁺ at about 1 ppm, and 13 nm of excitation tuning switches the emission from the blue-green Tb-like lines to the red Eu³⁺ lines. A 1200 °C anneal lowers the defect band fivefold. A nondestructive optical screen for substrates.

    Third-harmonic deep-UV source · I–V responsivity · power calibration

    UV photoresponsivity of GeO₂ photodetectors

    The solar-blind response of ultrawide-bandgap GeO2 detectors across 233 to 267 nm, measured with a tunable third-harmonic deep-UV source: 2.1 mA/W at 233 nm under 10 V. I aligned the source, calibrated the power and did the responsivity analysis.
