    Engineering Spin States in Si QD–Organic Hybrids

    01

    Why TR-EPR

    Si quantum dot–molecule hybrids generate long-lived triplet excitons, but nearly everything known about their triplet energy transfer comes from optical measurements, which do not resolve spin. Time-resolved electron paramagnetic resonance (TR-EPR) does: it can tell a triplet formed by spin–orbit intersystem crossing from one formed by charge recombination or direct energy transfer.

    The conjugates pair 2.6 nm dodecane-capped Si QDs with three anthracene derivatives covalently bound through different bridges. The 480 nm excitation is absorbed only by the QD, so any triplet on the molecule came from the dot.

    9-ethylanthracene (9EA, flexible single bond), 9-vinylanthracene (9VA, π-conjugated C=C linker) and the larger 9,10-bis(phenylethynyl)anthracene (BPEA). The Nano Letters paper covers 9EA and 9VA; BPEA is from the ESP-2025 poster.

    02

    The emissive spectrum

    TR-EPR at 20 K shows the molecular anthracene triplet, and its electron spin polarization is fully emissive: the sublevels are populated from the top down, p(T₊₁) > p(T₀) > p(T₋₁).

    Si QD–9VA at 9.5 GHz, 20 K, 480 nm excitation: the half-field ΔmS = 2 line near 168 mT and the whole ΔmS = 1 envelope, about 270 to 395 mT, point the same way.

    Emission (E) is plotted downward and absorption (A) upward, dashed line at zero; the narrow line marked * near 346 mT is a sharp radical or defect signal. Spin–orbit intersystem crossing and radical-pair recombination both give mixed absorptive/emissive patterns, so the Si QD's dark exciton must form with its T₊₁ level overpopulated, and Dexter transfer delivers that polarization to the molecule intact.

    03

    Linker and delocalization

    The zero-field splitting D measures the separation of the two unpaired electrons, so it reports where the triplet sits. Simulations of the spectra give D for each conjugate.

    D = 2085 MHz for 9EA, essentially identical to free anthracene (2184 MHz), so the triplet stays localized on the chromophore. The π-conjugated vinyl bridge of 9VA lowers D to 1775 MHz, so the triplet delocalizes across the linker, and the extended conjugation of BPEA spreads it furthest (D = 1600 MHz).

    Si QDs are not only light absorbers but triplet donors with a defined spin polarization. Linker chemistry sets where the triplet sits and the QD's exciton physics sets its spin polarization, the combination hybrid materials need for photon upconversion, spintronics and quantum information.
