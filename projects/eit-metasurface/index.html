    EIT-Like Terahertz Metasurface: a Full-Wave Design Study

    01

    The transparency window

    Two coupled resonators whose responses interfere destructively open a narrow transparency window between two absorption dips, the metamaterial counterpart of electromagnetically induced transparency. I rebuilt the aluminum C-arc and S-arc cell of Zhang et al. [1] in Ansys HFSS to reproduce the window, then asked two fabrication questions the paper did not address.

    A single resonator has one broad dip. The coupled cell transmits again through a window at 0.81 THz, where the steep phase gives a peak group delay of 5.3 ps.

    Floquet-port excitation, periodic boundaries, ΔS < 0.02 convergence. Dashed: a single resonator alone; blue: the coupled cell; red: the group delay from the exported S-parameters.

    02

    Validation

    Before changing anything I checked the model against the published device, feature by feature.

    Dip 1 lands within 0.7% of the published value and the peak group delay within 3.6% (5.3 ps against 5.5 ps); the window and dip 2 come out 6.9% and 10.9% low.

    The red-shift of the higher features comes from the bulk polyimide εr = 3.1 assigned in HFSS and cancels out of the relative comparisons below. A two-oscillator model fitted to the HFSS transmission gives γ₁ = 0.110 THz, γ₂ = 0.400 THz, κ = 0.338 THz and δ = 0.283 THz.

    Feature Zhang et al. This work (HFSS) Δ
    Dip 1 0.70 THz 0.705 THz +0.7%
    Transparency window 0.87 THz 0.81 THz −6.9%
    Dip 2 1.19 THz 1.06 THz −10.9%

    04

    Mode switching

    The surface fields show where the window comes from: at each dip the energy sits in one resonator, and the window lies between them where neither is strongly excited.

    At 0.70 THz (dip 1) the S-arc carries the field; at 1.10 THz (dip 2) it has moved to the C-arc.

    HFSS electric field 1 µm above the metasurface, cropped to one unit cell; the reference paper shows the same pattern at its dip, window and dip frequencies.

    05

    The sweeps

    With the model validated I asked what the paper did not: how much aluminum the resonators need when the skin depth at 1 THz is about 84 nm, whether polyimide is the right substrate, and whether a graphene monolayer can switch the window.

    100 nm of aluminum is enough, half the original device's metal: window transmission 0.717 against 0.612 at 50 nm, then 0.711 to 0.766 up to 500 nm. BCB lifts the window 14% over polyimide (Parylene-C 13%); gating graphene from the Dirac point to 0.5 eV drops it from 0.710 to 0.487, a 45.8% modulation depth.

    Substrates at 100 nm: polyimide tan δ = 0.05, SU-8 0.04, Parylene-C 0.003, BCB 0.005; SU-8 gains nothing because its loss cancels its higher permittivity. Graphene enters HFSS as an impedance boundary from the intraband Kubo conductivity; 45.8% is Tmin-normalized, 31.4% on a Tmax basis.
