    Optically Tunable THz Metasurface Absorber

    Seyedeh Shadi Mirmohammadi, Steve Blair and Berardi Sensale-Rodriguez, University of Utah.

    01

    The idea

    Fe-doped β-Ga2O3 is an ultrawide-bandgap crystal that an 808 nm laser can make conductive through its Fe acceptor states. Put a resonator array on it and the pump tunes the whole array, with no contacts, gates or heaters.

    The Fe level sits about 0.85 eV below the conduction band; the published photoconductivity reaches ~25 S/m at 2 W of 808 nm light [1].

    Unit cell: a gold H-shaped resonator (P 150, a 120, w 15, g 5 µm, 0.2 µm Au) on 724 µm of Fe:β-Ga₂O₃ over a gold ground plane. The ground plane blocks transmission, so the incident power is either reflected or absorbed.

    02

    Simulated switching

    In HFSS, raising the substrate conductivity from 0 to 25 S/m overcouples the resonator: the absorption peak at 300 GHz flattens and the surface turns from an absorber into a mirror.

    95.8% absorbed in the dark state, 17.0% at 25 S/m: 82.2% absorption modulation and 95.0% modulation of the reflected power.

    Drag the pump-power control. Every curve is the HFSS export for one of the 12 conductivity states (280 to 320 GHz, adaptive mesh at 0.32 THz, ΔS < 0.01). The resonance does not shift as σ rises; it flattens, and most of the change happens between 0.5 and 10 S/m.

    03

    Measured photoconductivity

    Before building on the published number I measured our own crystals: CW THz photomixing through a bare Fe:β-Ga2O3 plate while the 808 nm pump steps up and then switches off.

    6.8 S/m at 20 W. The 463 GHz signal drops by 86% and is back to 90% of its pump-off level 37 s after turn-off.

    Conductivity from the thin-film relation over the plate thickness; transmission normalized to the pump-off mean; pump steps 5, 10, 15 and 20 W. Photoexcitation from the Fe level is sub-picosecond, so a recovery over tens of seconds points to heating. Crystals from the groups of Prof. Kai Fu and Prof. Michael Scarpulla.

    04

    The fabricated array

    The ITO version is in fabrication. The latest array came through lift-off with every cell intact, and its THz transmission matches the HFSS model with nothing fitted on the array.

    0.5 µm of sputtered ITO at 20 Ω/sq; a 99, w 15.5, g 19 µm as fabricated; measured transmission within 0.04 rms of HFSS over 300 to 650 GHz.

    Written on the DWL 66+ laser writer and lifted off from an LOR 10B / S1813 bilayer (AZ 300 MIF develop to a 2.5 µm undercut, O₂ descum). Earlier arrays lost cells because the undercut was only 0.7 to 0.9 µm on 1.2 µm of resist. At 20 Ω/sq the resonance is heavily damped; the next steps are to anneal the ITO, add the ground plane and measure under the pump.

    05

    The ITO electrode

    Gold blocks the pump, so the device that can be illuminated through its own pattern uses ITO for the resonators and the ground plane. On the (010) cut the crystal is birefringent, which gives separate TE and TM channels.

    Four channels switch by more than 10 dB (TE at 238 and 682 GHz, TM at 346 and 419 GHz) at 4.0 to 4.7 dB insertion loss. Sputtered ITO: 19 → 4.9 Ω/sq at 384 nm after a forming-gas anneal, σ ≈ 5.3 × 10⁵ S/m.

    Recipe on the Denton Discovery 18: 50 W DC, Ar/O₂ 28.3/10.3 sccm, 3.86 mTorr, 34 min 30 s, thickness within 1% of the 380 nm target; the 4.9 Ω/sq reproduced over three probe points per sample with under 3% spread. As-deposited films read about twice their DC sheet resistance at THz frequencies.
