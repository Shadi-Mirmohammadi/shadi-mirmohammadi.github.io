    Research Experience

    • Jan 2026–Present

      Graduate Research Assistant

      Blair Lab, University of Utah, with the Sensale-Rodriguez group
      • Designed and simulated optically tunable THz/mmWave metasurface modulators on Fe-doped β-Ga₂O₃ using full-wave 3D EM simulation (Ansys HFSS); in reflection, simulations show four TE/TM channels between 238 and 682 GHz that switch by at least 10 dB at 4 to 5 dB insertion loss.
      • Performed S-parameter extraction and parametric optimization across 12 conductivity states, identifying critical-coupling conditions yielding 82.2% absorption modulation and 95.0% reflected-power modulation at 300 GHz (H-SRR design; IRMMW-THz 2026).
      • Developed ITO transparent-electrode process end-to-end (Denton Discovery 18): thickness-on-demand sputter recipes and anneals reaching 4.9 Ω/sq at 384 nm (σ ≈ 5.3 × 10⁵ S/m).
      • Qualified on maskless laser lithography (Heidelberg DWL 66+); designed, wrote, and developed own SRR mask layouts — first SRR array patterned in 400 nm ITO.
      • Performed CW-THz transmission spectroscopy (CW photomixing spectrometer) on ITO/sapphire films to extract THz-frequency sheet resistance; reconciling THz-extracted vs. DC four-point-probe conductivity.
      • Built Python data-analysis pipelines (pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib) automating HFSS post-processing and measurement analysis; published open-source on GitHub.
      • Characterized excitation-selective Eu³⁺ emission switching and annealing-suppressible defect signatures in GdScO₃ single-crystal substrates via UV photoluminescence spectroscopy; first-author manuscript in preparation for Optical Materials Express; accepted poster, MRS Fall 2026.
      • Analyzed UV photoresponsivity of GeO₂ photodetectors in a cross-group collaboration with Dr. Kai Fu's lab (Imteaz Rahaman), applying beam/detector-area corrections and calibrated power measurement.
    • 2023–Dec 2025

      Graduate Researcher

      Department of Chemistry (Tang and Luo groups), University of Utah
      • Synthesized zinc-blende CdS quantum dots across a size series and NDI ligands, and functionalized Si quantum dots with anthracene and NDI ligands by hydrosilylation under air-free (Schlenk) conditions.
      • Formed CdS quantum-dot gels by potentiostatic electrochemical gelation and characterized their fractal structure by lab SAXS (Beaucage model).
      • Extended CdSe–anthracene triplet–triplet-annihilation upconversion to CdSe nanoplatelets and showed that the carboxylic-acid anchor position (9- vs 1- vs 2-ACA) changes upconversion yield by an order of magnitude (0.43% / 0.10% / 0.03%); measured triplet energy transfer by nanosecond transient absorption.
      • Studied spin-correlated radical pairs in Si quantum dot–NDI conjugates by time-resolved EPR with Argonne National Laboratory (first-author poster, ESP 2025) and contributed to the Nano Letters 2026 study of spin-polarized anthracene triplets on Si quantum dots.
