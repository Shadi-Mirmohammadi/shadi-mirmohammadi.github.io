    Summary

    • Ph.D. student in Electrical & Computer Engineering at the University of Utah, working on optically tunable terahertz metasurfaces: full-wave design in Ansys HFSS, ITO device fabrication in the cleanroom, and optical and THz measurement. M.S. in chemistry. Six peer-reviewed journal papers, two as co-first author, including Nano Letters 2026; first-author paper and poster at IRMMW-THz 2026.

    Selected Results

    • Measured 86% switching of a 463 GHz THz beam through Fe:β-Ga₂O₃ under an 808 nm pump, with recovery to 90% of the pump-off level 37 s after turn-off; photoconductivity up to 6.8 S/m at 20 W.
    • Designed a THz metasurface absorber in Ansys HFSS with 95.8% dark-state absorption, 82.2% absorption modulation and 95.0% reflected-power modulation at 300 GHz, and an ITO version with four TE/TM channels switching by at least 10 dB (first-author paper and poster, IRMMW-THz 2026).
    • Fabricated the first ITO H-shaped resonator array on Fe:β-Ga₂O₃ with every cell intact after lift-off; its measured THz transmission matches HFSS to 0.04 rms over 300 to 650 GHz.
    • Developed an ITO electrode process that reaches 4.9 Ω/sq at 384 nm (σ ≈ 5.3 × 10⁵ S/m) with a thickness-on-demand sputter recipe (within 1% of target).
    • Showed excitation-selective Eu³⁺ emission in nominally undoped GdScO₃ substrates (491/612 nm line ratio from 1.03 to 0.13 for 250 to 263 nm excitation); first-author manuscript, poster accepted at MRS Fall 2026.
    • Six peer-reviewed journal papers, two as co-first author, including Nano Letters 2026; Best Poster Award at ESP-2025.

    Research Experience

    • Jan 2026–Present

      Graduate Research Assistant

      Blair Lab, University of Utah, with the Sensale-Rodriguez group
      • Designed an optically switchable THz metasurface absorber on Fe-doped β-Ga₂O₃ in Ansys HFSS: 95.8% absorption in the dark state, 82.2% absorption modulation and 95.0% reflected-power modulation at 300 GHz across a 0 to 25 S/m photoconductivity sweep. First-author paper and poster, IRMMW-THz 2026.
      • Redesigned the absorber with transparent ITO resonators and ground plane so the 808 nm pump reaches the crystal through the pattern; simulations show four TE/TM reflection channels between 238 and 682 GHz that switch by at least 10 dB at 4 to 5 dB insertion loss.
      • Fabricated ITO H-shaped resonator arrays with 15 µm lines on glass and Fe:β-Ga₂O₃ by maskless laser lithography (Heidelberg DWL 66+), LOR/S1813 bilayer lift-off and DC sputtering. The latest Ga₂O₃ array came through lift-off with every cell intact, and its THz transmission matches HFSS to 0.04 rms from 300 to 650 GHz using the film's DC conductivity and the measured dimensions.
      • Traced incomplete lift-off on Ga₂O₃ to an LOR undercut that was too small; a longer develop to a 2.5 to 2.7 µm undercut and an O₂ descum before deposition fixed it on the next device.
      • Developed the ITO transparent-electrode process on the Denton Discovery 18: thickness-on-demand sputter recipes (384 nm, within 1% of target) and anneals reaching 4.9 Ω/sq (σ ≈ 5.3 × 10⁵ S/m); compared four-point-probe sheet resistance with values extracted from THz transmission.
      • Measured THz transmission from 0.2 to 1 THz on a CW photomixing spectrometer. Under an 808 nm pump, Fe:β-Ga₂O₃ cut the transmitted THz signal by 86% at 463 GHz and recovered to 90% of the pump-off level within 37 s of turn-off; the photoconductivity reached 6.8 S/m at 20 W.
      • Built HFSS material models for Ga₂O₃ from published anisotropic permittivity plus permittivity and loss fitted to measured THz transmission, bringing simulated and measured substrate transmission to within about 0.05 rms. Parametric and crystal-cut studies: (010) and (100) reach 98% dark-state absorption, while the (001) cut cannot be critically coupled.
      • Analyzed THz and HFSS data in MATLAB and Python (envelope-ratio transmission, thin-film conductivity fits, HFSS post-processing); the analysis code is public on GitHub.
      • Characterized excitation-selective Eu³⁺ emission and annealing-suppressible defect signatures in nominally undoped GdScO₃ substrates by deep-UV photoluminescence from 77 K to room temperature: tuning the excitation from 250 to 263 nm cuts the 491/612 nm line ratio from 1.03 to 0.13. First-author manuscript in preparation for Optical Materials Express; poster accepted at MRS Fall 2026.
      • Aligned and operated a third-harmonic deep-UV source and calibrated optical power for responsivity measurements of MOCVD-grown GeO₂ photodetectors (2.1 mA/W at 233 nm) with Dr. Kai Fu's lab (Imteaz Rahaman); equal-contribution co-author of the manuscript.
      • Measured power- and temperature-dependent 808 nm photoconduction in Fe:β-Ga₂O₃ switches at 300 V to 10 kV and 25 to 100 °C (co-author, 10 kV photoconductive switch manuscript).
      • Measured and modeled spectroscopic ellipsometry (J.A. Woollam VASE, WVASE) of GdScO₃ crystals, doped SiC wafers and Glassomer layers with Cauchy and oscillator models.
    • 2023–Dec 2025

      Graduate Researcher

      Department of Chemistry (Tang and Luo groups), University of Utah
      • Synthesized zinc-blende CdS quantum dots across a size series and NDI ligands, and functionalized Si quantum dots with anthracene and NDI ligands by hydrosilylation under air-free (Schlenk) conditions.
      • Formed CdS quantum-dot gels by potentiostatic electrochemical gelation and characterized their fractal structure by lab SAXS (Beaucage model).
      • Extended CdSe–anthracene triplet–triplet-annihilation upconversion to CdSe nanoplatelets and showed that the carboxylic-acid anchor position (9- vs 1- vs 2-ACA) changes the upconversion yield by an order of magnitude (0.43% / 0.10% / 0.03%); measured triplet energy transfer by nanosecond transient absorption and photoluminescence decays, with kinetics analysis in Igor Pro.
      • Studied spin-correlated radical pairs in Si quantum dot–NDI conjugates by time-resolved EPR with Argonne National Laboratory (first-author poster, ESP 2025, Best Poster Award) and contributed to the Nano Letters 2026 study of spin-polarized anthracene triplets on Si quantum dots.

    Techniques

    EM simulation and device design Ansys HFSS 2025 R2: Floquet-port unit cells with periodic (primary/secondary) boundaries, adaptive and multi-frequency meshing, anisotropic and frequency-dependent (dataset) material models fitted to measured THz transmission, impedance boundaries (layered sheets, graphene from the Kubo conductivity), Optimetrics parametric sweeps (period, linewidth, arm length, gap, substrate thickness, crystal cut, conductivity), S-parameter export and post-processing. Absorber critical coupling, TE/TM channel design on birefringent substrates, ITO thickness optimization (extinction ratio and insertion loss), coupled-resonator (EIT) modeling, group-delay extraction from the S21 phase, coupled-oscillator fits by differential evolution.
    Cleanroom fabrication (University of Utah Nanofab) Maskless laser lithography (Heidelberg DWL 66+; layout design, dose and power selection, 15 µm features), LOR 10B / S1813 bilayer resist process (spin coating, HMDS, softbake, AZ 300 MIF develop with undercut control to 2.5–2.7 µm, undercut inspection), O₂ plasma descum (Technics PE II-A), DC magnetron sputtering of ITO (Denton Discovery 18; recipe development for thickness on demand, Ar/O₂ flow and pressure control, heated-stage trials), bilayer lift-off (acetone and MIF soaks), rapid thermal annealing (AccuThermo AW 610; forming gas and Ar), Cl₂/Ar ICP etch trials (PlasmaTherm SLR), wet processing (TMAH develop, resist strip, solvent cleans), thermal evaporation, substrate polishing with diamond lapping film, linewidth-bias characterization from sputter shadowing.
    Metrology Stylus profilometry (KLA-Tencor P-20H; thickness, step height, roughness), four-point probe sheet resistance, Keyence VHX digital microscopy (dimensions, defect counts), optical microscopy, spectroscopic ellipsometry (J.A. Woollam VASE, WVASE; multi-angle Ψ/Δ, Cauchy and Lorentz/Cody–Lorentz oscillator models, reference-sample calibration), lab SAXS (Beaucage fractal fits), AFM, analysis of XRD rocking curves and LA-ICP-MS trace-element data.
    Optical and THz measurement CW THz photomixing spectroscopy (0.2–1 THz: transmission, envelope-ratio analysis, Fabry–Pérot thickness and permittivity extraction, Tinkham thin-film sheet resistance, photoconductivity under 808 nm pumping, polarization-dependent anisotropy), VDI 300 GHz source measurements, high-power CW 808 nm pumping (to 20 W; Class 4 laser safety), tunable deep-UV source by third-harmonic generation of a Ti:sapphire laser (alignment, 233–280 nm tuning, Thorlabs power-meter calibration), deep-UV photoluminescence and transmission with an LN₂ cryostat (77 K to room temperature), NIST-lamp spectrometer calibration, excitation-emission mapping and CIE color analysis, nanosecond transient absorption (triplet-transfer kinetics), photoluminescence decays, UV–Vis, FTIR/ATR-IR, Raman, circular dichroism (Jasco spectropolarimeter), photodetector I–V and responsivity (dark/illuminated sweeps, beam-area correction), photoconductive-switch measurements from 300 V to 10 kV and 25 to 100 °C.
    Synthesis and materials Hot-injection synthesis of zinc-blende CdS quantum dots (size series), ligand exchange (thioglycolic acid), Si quantum dot functionalization by hydrosilylation, anthracene and NDI ligand synthesis under air-free (Schlenk) conditions, potentiostatic electrochemical gelation of quantum-dot gels.
    Software and analysis Python (NumPy, SciPy, pandas, Matplotlib), MATLAB (THz envelope analysis), Igor Pro (kinetics), LaTeX, Fusion 360 CAD and 3D printing, Git and GitHub (public analysis repositories); curve fitting, thin-film conductivity modeling, data pipelines for HFSS and measurement post-processing.
